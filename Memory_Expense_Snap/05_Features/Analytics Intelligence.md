# Analytics Intelligence

Real-time Financial Insight Engine that transforms raw transaction logs into actionable, plain-English advice.

## Active Capabilities

- **Real-Time Financial Diagnosis**:
  - Automatically calculates category dominance, weekly velocity spikes, and budget pace from the user's actual transaction history.
  - Highlights concrete savings opportunities (e.g., 15% reduction in top spend category).
- **Sleek Obsidian Glassmorphic Card**:
  - Clean, quiet-luxury visual design with dark obsidian surface, amber gold accents, and subtle breathing financial trend icon (`PhosphorIcons.chartLineUp`).
  - No clichéd AI sparkle stars or technical jargon.
  - Clean status pills (`SAVINGS OPPORTUNITY`, `SPENDING SPIKE`, `ON TRACK`, `SPENDING OVERVIEW`, `NEW MONTH`).
- **Interactive Financial Diagnosis Sheet**:
  - Draggable bottom sheet with full spending breakdown, category concentration bar, and personalized action plan.
  - 3 concrete, plain-English action items with interactive toggle checkmarks.
  - "Refresh Analysis" action querying Google Gemini 2.5 Flash with seamless local heuristic fallbacks.
- **Dynamic Trend & Velocity Calculations**:
  - Day-over-day, week-over-week, and month-over-month comparisons (`_getTrendDelta()`, `_getPeakStat()`).
  - Interactive activity matrix with scrollable 12-month calendar picker (January through December).
