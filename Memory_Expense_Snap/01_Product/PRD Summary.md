# PRD Summary

Kharcha is a Flutter personal finance app for Pakistani users. It is AI-first, voice-first, and optimized for fast expense entry.

## MVP

- Complete onboarding/personalization.
- Add expense through manual, voice UI, or scan UI path.
- Save to one central transaction source.
- Update [[Dashboard]], [[History]], and [[Analytics]].
- Preserve [[Design System]].

## V1

- [[Firebase Architecture]]
- [[Voice Logging]] with Gemini parsing.
- [[OCR Scanning]] with ML Kit.
- [[Notifications Feature]]
- [[Exports]]

## V2

- [[Google Sheets Sync]]
- Monthly AI story.
- Anomaly alerts.
- Habit patterns.
- Budget forecasting.
