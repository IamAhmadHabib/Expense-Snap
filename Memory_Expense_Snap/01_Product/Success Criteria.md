# Success Criteria

## MVP Success

- Expense can be saved to a real source of truth.
- Saved expense appears in [[History]].
- [[Dashboard]] totals update.
- [[Analytics]] reflects saved data.
- UI follows [[Color Tokens]] and [[Screen Design Rules]].

## Launch Success

- Auth and cloud storage work.
- Voice and OCR flows are real.
- Export/sync features are trustworthy.
- App feels premium and stable.
