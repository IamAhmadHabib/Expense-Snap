# Design System

Kharcha design is warm, premium, charcoal-and-amber, and quietly luxurious.

The app should feel crafted, calm, tactile, and trustworthy.

## Iconography Principles

- **No Cartoonish Clichés**: Avoid literal fast-food burgers or popcorn buckets. Use mature, architectural metaphors (`forkKnife`, `carSimple`, `tote`, `ticket`, `firstAidKit`, `receipt`, `bookOpen`, `basket`, `airplaneTilt`, `tag`).
- **Solid Silhouette & Weight**: Icons use `PhosphorIconsStyle.fill` at 24px so they have physical mass, clarity, and life rather than looking like thin wire skeletons.
- **Breathable Squircle Housing**: Never trap icons in heavy solid black boxes. Use 48×48 / 50×50 continuous-corner squircles with soft category-tinted pastel backgrounds and matching rich accent icons.
- **Selection Interaction**: In pickers and sheets, selected items illuminate with category pastel fills, accent borders, and subtle glows rather than turning pitch black.
